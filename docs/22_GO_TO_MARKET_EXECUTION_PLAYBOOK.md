# VestrenHQ — Go-to-Market Execution Playbook

**Status:** operational plan. Stages are gates, not calendar promises. Do not spend on scale before evidence supports it.

## Launch strategy
The first objective is to find a repeatable job, deliver a useful artifact, earn repeat use, and test willingness to pay with manageable support and cost—not maximize traffic.

## Stage 0 — readiness and sample
Before outreach, pick one segment/workflow from interview evidence; run it on a representative sanitized task; publish one accurate sample with sources; document limitations, data handling, caps, support and failure behavior; verify signup/onboarding; and confirm landing/support links. Do not accept payment until product, security, billing, and support gates pass.

Deliverables: sample artifact, one-page product explanation, demo script, feedback form, known-limitations page.

## Stage 1 — founder dogfood
Use Vestren on at least 10 real, non-sensitive tasks. Keep a fixed evaluation set with ordinary, ambiguous, and failure-prone inputs. Record source relevance, unsupported claims, completion/failure, recovery, time-to-useful-output, correction time, provider/infrastructure cost, and whether the artifact was used. Fix repeated failures before external invitations.

## Stage 2 — 3–5 design partners
Invite people with a recent, recurring problem. Agree on a bounded test, task, usage cap, limitations, support contact, and feedback session. Observe use rather than merely asking whether they like it.

Success evidence: at least three partners finish the same workflow; at least three return for a second real task within seven days; users confirm the deliverable was used; quality/cost data exist; no unresolved critical security/data-loss issue. These are internal thresholds, not universal benchmarks.

## Stage 3 — paid pilot
Only after applicable release gates pass, define workflow/scope, included runs or credits, what consumes a credit, total price/tax treatment, expiry, refund/cancellation, response window, and support channel. Verify payment callbacks, idempotency, entitlement, refunds and reconciliation. Use a controlled transaction before public checkout.

Start with one-time prepaid packs or a fixed-scope pilot. Do not promise automatic recurring billing unless provider support and the complete renewal lifecycle are verified.

## Stage 4 — controlled public beta
Open only after public auth, tenant isolation, persistence, export/deletion, usage controls, accurate policies, support, payment if sold, incident response, and rollback are proven. Start with a limited cohort and caps. Keep kill switches for each paid provider and new signups.

## Acquisition experiments
Run one experiment at a time:
- Direct outreach: offer a relevant sample from public information; do not imply a relationship or misuse private data.
- Workflow demo: show source ledger and exported brief, not only a chat transcript.
- Practical content: share a reproducible workflow with an example.
- Referral: ask only after the user confirms value.
- Community channel: follow community rules and platform access limits.

For each experiment define segment, hypothesis, message, sample, CTA, time budget, success threshold, result, and next decision before starting. Do not spam or buy traffic before conversion/retention are understood.

## Funnel and weekly review
Track reached → qualified conversation → signup → first workflow → successful artifact → artifact used → seven-day repeat → paid offer → payment → refund/cancellation. Review cost per useful artifact and support time alongside conversion. Pause channels that attract unqualified traffic or increase cost without repeat use.

## Launch decision
- Go: workflow reliable, repeat use demonstrated, support manageable, cost bounded, legal/payment surfaces accurate.
- Hold: missing security, provider, support, payment, or deployment evidence.
- Narrow: users value one subtask but not the broader workflow.
- Pivot: no repeated pain or credible payment intent after a documented test.

No public announcement overrides a failed P0 gate.
